Everything the pantry needs

A rules-based AI you can actually read, wrapped in an interface that feels like a farmers-market shelf tag.

Local Auth

Sign up and log in — all stored safely in your browser via localStorage. No cloud, no tracking.

Inventory Tracking

Add products with category, quantity, and date. Search, filter and sort a growing pantry with ease.

How data flows

  1. 1

    Add product

    You enter name, category, quantity and date added.

  2. 2

    AI analyze()

    Rules engine returns predicted expiry, days remaining and risk.

  3. 3

    Persist

    Everything is saved to localStorage under your account.

  4. 4

    Surface

    Dashboard, inventory and donation views react in real time.

  5. 5

    Act

    Use, donate or discard — activity feed keeps the record.
